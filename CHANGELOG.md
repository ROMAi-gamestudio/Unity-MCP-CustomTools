- Build: `build-platform-get`, `build-platform-set`, `build-scenes-list`, `build-scenes-set`, `build-settings-get`, `build-settings-set`, `build-execute`.
- Profiler: `profiler-start`, `profiler-stop`, `profiler-status`, `profiler-get-frame-timing`, `profiler-get-counters`.
- Smoke: `smoke-greet`.
